## <span class="no">2</span> How the model writes a reply

The model generates a reply by **predicting one token at a time**. Tokens can be whole words or parts of words.

<div class="nextw"><div class="nw-q">For lunch I’ll have a <span>?</span></div><div class="nw-a"><div><b>sandwich</b><i style="width:70%"></i><em>High</em></div><div><b>salad</b><i style="width:22%"></i><em>Medium</em></div><div><b>refrigerator</b><i style="width:4%"></i><em>Low</em></div></div><p class="nw-c">The bars show example likelihoods for three possible next words.</p></div>

To make a prediction, the model weighs which earlier tokens matter. This mechanism is called **attention**.

<div class="attn">
<div class="at-row"><span class="w">10 tokens</span><span class="x">→</span><span class="c"><b>100</b> pairs</span></div>
<div class="at-row"><span class="w">1,000 tokens</span><span class="x">→</span><span class="c"><b>1 million</b> pairs</span></div>
</div>

If you count every possible pair in standard full attention, the count grows with the **square** of the input length.<br>
With a long input, the model can have more trouble picking out what matters.

Anthropic, the maker of Claude, describes attention as a **limited resource**.

## <span class="no">3</span> What happens in long chats

The **context window** is the amount of information a model can use at once.

<div class="facts">
<div><b>A long chat can lose its beginning</b>The details you set at the start can be dropped or summarized.</div>
<div><b>The middle can get missed</b>Even within the limit, models may find information at the start and end more readily.</div>
<div><b>A new chat has its own context</b>It may receive selected memories, but another assistant won’t automatically have the conversation.</div>
</div>

## <span class="no">4</span> Three ways to keep your place

<ul class="checks">
<li><svg><use href="#i-check"/></svg><span><b>When a chat gets too long</b>, start a fresh one and ask the assistant to read your work log first.</span></li>
<li><svg><use href="#i-check"/></svg><span><b>When an instruction matters</b>, put it at the beginning or end of your request.</span></li>
<li><svg><use href="#i-check"/></svg><span><b>When you share files</b>, point the assistant to the ones it needs for the task.</span></li>
