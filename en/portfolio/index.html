Portfolio

Party roomAn app for booking messages, guest questions, and refund requests

Problem
For each Naver booking, I copied payment and parking instructions, calculated extra guest fees, checked bank deposits, and followed up on unanswered photo review requests.
Bookings could arrive at any time, so I had to keep checking my phone.
I also answered questions about parking, waste, and dishes, and verified payments for extensions and extra guests.
Outcome
The app reads bookings, sends instructions and photo review offers, matches security deposits, and schedules confirmation and reminder texts. It answers common questions about parking, fees, waste, dishes, and consent forms for minors. It confirms requests for extensions, extra guests, or parking once payment matches. It also adjusts refunds when the guest count drops. When a Naver booking is confirmed, it blocks the same time slot on SpaceCloud. Cleaners get a dedicated view of the schedule and towel and water counts. I handle individual replies, message delivery checks, and refund transfers. I no longer have to watch my phone for every booking.

Status as of October 25, 2026. I verified payment matching and automatic replies on a live parking request. Extensions and extra guest requests were still awaiting verification with real bookings.

Coin karaokeSelf-service redemption for unused songs

Coin karaoke · Airbnb · Party roomsTime tracking and payroll automation

Problem
I cross-checked shifts for more than 10 part-time staff using KakaoTalk and a paper log, then copied the records into Excel to calculate pay, weekly paid leave allowances, and taxes.
Even with Excel formulas, different rates for shift covers and exceptions to weekly allowances took time to calculate.

Coin karaokeSelf-service karaoke recording with direct microphone audio

Problem
I was looking for a new revenue stream and saw that guests wanted to record their karaoke performances.
Phone recordings often had muddy audio, poor lighting, and awkward camera angles.
I needed an in-room system where guests could pay and record themselves with dedicated lighting.
Outcome
Guests pay by QR code and start recording from the room. The lights turn on, and the video records audio directly from the microphone.Guests can view and save the video on their phones when it’s ready.I stopped offering the service after beta testing.

PublishingAn international news blog that published itself at 7 a.m.

Problem
I wanted to read international perspectives alongside Korean news and tried running an ad-supported WordPress blog.
I wanted to spend as little time as possible gathering sources and writing.
Outcome
At 7 a.m. each day, the workflow published an illustrated story to two WordPress sites, one in English and one in Korean. It rotated among economics, media, and technology. Publishing took zero minutes of manual work.I found that search terms, ranking, and traffic mattered more to this model than publishing volume. I decided to stop operating the sites.
